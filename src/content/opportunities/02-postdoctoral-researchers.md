Exceptional candidates may email Prof. Rosen with the subject line “Postdoc Opportunity,” a CV, and a short description of research interests. Please note that Prof. Rosen is not able to respond to all inquiries.

Candidates are strongly encouraged to apply for university-sponsored fellowships when applicable. For applications requiring nomination, include the fellowship, a CV, and a few sentences about the research you hope to pursue.
