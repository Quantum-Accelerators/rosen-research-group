The group is awarded an NSF grant ($725,000) for a project entitled “SINAPSE: Scalable Infrastructure for AI-coupled Predictive Simulation Enhancement” for a joint collaboration with Dr. Shantenu Jha, Dr. Eliu Huerta, and Dr. Rommie Amaro.
